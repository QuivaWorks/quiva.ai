**Account → Email sending** is where you control the email your account sends: the templates flows and Abbie send from, what became of each message, and the addresses that will not be written to. The tab is for account admins.

It has three cards, each with an **Open** button for its own page, plus your [sending domain](#sending-domain) setup.

## Email templates

A template is a subject and an HTML body in your branding, with `{tags}` for the parts that change per email, such as `{customer.first_name}`. Flows and Abbie send from a **published** template. Only the published version is ever sent.

<Frame caption="The Email templates page: each template with its key, status, tag count and last update.">
  <img src="/images/essentials/email-templates.png" alt="The Email templates page listing three templates with their key, a Published or Draft status, tag count and updated time" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Open **Email templates**. Either click **Ask Abbie to create one**, which opens a chat with a prompt ready for you, or click **New template**, give it a **Name** and **Description**, and choose **Create and edit**. Abbie can work from a written brief, a reference image or a reference document, and writes the result as a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview it">
    Click a row. The preview renders against the template's sample data. Switch between **Draft** and **Published**, **Desktop** and **Phone**, and HTML or plain text. Any tag the sample data does not answer is called out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the draft">
    Choose **Edit** to change the **Subject**, the HTML, the plain-text version and the sample data. **Save draft** keeps it unpublished. You can also attach files, which are sent with every email once the template is published, up to 6 MB in total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    An admin publishes the draft. **Discard** throws the draft away and leaves the published version as it was.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Creating and editing drafts is open to any member. Publishing, discarding and deleting need an admin.
</Note>

Brand details such as your logo and colours come from the account through `{account.*}` and `{sender.*}` tags. A send is refused, naming the tags, if any tag cannot be filled. Deleting a template that a flow still uses is allowed; the flow's next send fails and names the missing template.

Use **Record data** in the preview to merge a record set's fields into the sample data.

## Email delivery

**Email delivery** shows what became of each email in the last 90 days, newest first. The columns are **To**, **Subject**, **Status**, **Type**, **Template** and **Sent**. Filter by status or by recipient, and open a row for its full event history.

<Frame caption="The Email delivery log, newest first, with the status and recipient filters above it.">
  <img src="/images/essentials/email-delivery.png" alt="The Email delivery page with an Any status filter, a Recipient filter and a table of sent emails showing Delivered and Not sent statuses" />
</Frame>

Statuses you will see include **Delivered**, **Opened**, **Clicked**, **Delayed**, **Bounced**, **Dropped**, **Blocked**, **Marked as spam**, **Unsubscribed** and **Not sent**. **Not sent** covers mail withheld because the address is suppressed and mail refused before it left.

Two switches sit on the **Email delivery** card:

<ParamField path="Raise a task when an email fails" type="setting">
Pick a space and a task is created for each recipient whose email fails. Leave it on **No task** to turn it off. To react in other ways, such as a notification or a retry, use the [Email delivery trigger](/flows/triggers/email-delivery).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Track opens and clicks" type="switch">
Off by default. When on, QuivaWorks records when a recipient opens a message and when they click a link in marketing mail. Your account must tell recipients it does this. Sent and failed mail is always recorded, whatever this is set to.
</ParamField>

<Warning>
Tracking is off for every account until you turn it on, and **existing accounts must re-verify their sending domain** to receive open and click events. Until then the delivery page notes that tracking is off or missing events.
</Warning>

## Suppression list

The suppression list holds addresses that will not be written to. They get there automatically, or you can add them.

<Frame caption="The Suppression list page: look up an address, add one, and the note that the full list cannot be browsed.">
  <img src="/images/essentials/email-suppression.png" alt="The Suppression list page with a Look up an address form, an Add an address form with a Reason dropdown, and an Everything on the list section" />
</Frame>

| Reason | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Hard bounce** | The mail server rejected the address permanently. |
| **Marked as spam** | The recipient reported your mail as spam. |
| **Unsubscribed** | The person asked not to receive commercial mail. |
| **Do not contact** | Added deliberately. |
| **Added manually** | Added by you for another reason. |

On **Suppression list** you can:

- **Look up** an address to see whether it can be written to for each kind of mail.
- **Add** an address with a reason.
- **Lift** a suppression. You give a reason and type the address to confirm. Lift one only when you are sure the owner wants mail again: re-sending to a bounced or spam-reporting address damages your delivery reputation for everyone else you write to.

Mail to a suppressed address is not sent, and shows as **Not sent** in the delivery log.

## Sending domain

An account with no verified domain sends through the platform automatically, with the From name `<Account> via QuivaWorks`, so nothing is needed to get started. To send from your own domain, enter it under **Sending domain**, publish the DNS records shown (mail server, two DKIM keys and SPF) with whoever hosts your DNS, then choose **Check records**. Verification can take minutes or hours depending on the provider. Once verified, mail is sent from your domain.

## Sending from flows

- The flow [Send email step](/flows/steps/email) sends from a published template.
- The [Email delivery trigger](/flows/triggers/email-delivery) starts a flow when an email bounces, is blocked or reaches any other delivery event.
