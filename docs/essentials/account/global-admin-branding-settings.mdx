Three sections of the **Account** page apply to the whole account: **Branding**, which changes how the app looks, and **Global Instructions** and **Global Knowledge**, which every assistant in the account can inherit.

Open **Account** in the sidebar — depending on your account it sits at the top level or under **More** — then pick the section from the list on the left. The page is visible to the **root** and **admin** roles only.

## Branding

The **Account Branding** section has three independent blocks. Each one saves and clears on its own.

### App Name

Set a custom name for your app. This appears in the browser tab and other branded surfaces.

Type the name into the **App Name** field and click **Save App Name**.

### Favicon

Upload a square image for your browser tab icon. PNG, JPG or ICO, SVG, 32×32px or larger.

A small browser mock-up above the upload area previews the tab as your users will see it, using your app name and the icon you have chosen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an image">
    Drop a file on the upload area or click to browse. You will be offered a cropper before the file is accepted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Favicon**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To go back to the default icon, click **Remove**.

### Dark mode favicon

A second upload, **Dark mode favicon**, is shown to people whose browser is in dark mode. Without one, your favicon is used.

### Brand Colors

Set the primary and secondary colours for your branded interfaces.

<Frame caption="Brand colors with Use separate dark theme colors on: the light and dark theme colours, Text on primary, and a live preview of each.">
  <img src="/images/account-branding.png" alt="The Brand colors card with Light theme and Dark theme columns, each with Primary color, Secondary color and Text on primary, and Light and Dark previews" />
</Frame>

Pick a **Primary Color** and a **Secondary Color**, then click **Save Colors**. **Remove** clears both back to the defaults.

- **Text on primary** sets the colour of text drawn on top of your primary colour, such as button labels.
- **Use separate dark theme colors** reveals **Dark theme primary** and **Dark theme secondary**, used when someone is in dark mode.

### Document theme

Branding has two tabs: **Application**, for everything above, and **Documents**, for the document theme. The document theme styles the documents and slide decks Abbie generates from scratch. For a fixed document with your own layout, such as an engagement letter or a proposal, upload it as a template instead and it is filled in.

- **Import from a document**: drop a branded `.docx`, `.pptx`, `.potx` or `.xlsx` file and the fonts, colours, chart colours and cover are read out of it. Nothing is saved until you review the result and click **Save document theme**. A font that cannot be embedded in a generated file is swapped for a close match, and the page tells you which.
- **Fonts and page**: heading, body and monospace fonts, body text size, page size, orientation, margin and slide size.
- **Colours**: accent, heading, body and muted colours.
- **Chart colours**: a list of colours used in series order. Add, remove and reorder them. **Gain colour** and **Loss colour** mark rises and falls.
- **Slides**: a **Cover colour** or a cover image for the title slide (PNG or JPG, up to 1 MiB), **Slide logo position** (bottom right or top right) and **Heading case** (as written or upper case).
- **Word template**, **Footer text** and **Hide page numbers** for documents.

**Document logo** sets the logo placed on generated documents and slides.

## Global Assistant Instructions

Define a set of global instructions that will be automatically applied to every interaction with assistants.

<Warning>
This feature needs to be enabled on each assistant that you want to use the global instructions. Writing global instructions does not on its own change how an existing assistant behaves.
</Warning>

Global instructions are the right place for standards that hold across the whole account, for example:

- Keep a professional, direct tone.
- Always check the source before quoting a figure.
- Hand over to a person when someone asks for one.

Keep them short. Behaviour that belongs to one job belongs in that assistant's own instructions instead.

## Global Knowledge

Upload or select files that you want to be available as knowledge for all assistants where the Global Knowledge feature is enabled.

Supported file types are **PDF**, **CSV**, **TXT** and **MD**. For best results, we recommend keeping individual file sizes under 5MB.

<Note>
Global Knowledge takes files. Pasting text and linking to a URL are not supported here.
</Note>

Typical content is material every assistant should be able to quote from — an internal glossary, a policy document, a standard price list.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can change these settings?">
    Only the root and admin roles can open the **Account** page at all. [About roles →](/essentials/users/roles-permissions)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why isn't my assistant using the global instructions?">
    Global instructions and global knowledge are opt-in per assistant. Enable the feature on the assistant itself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I go back to the default branding?">
    Yes, for the favicon and the colours — each block has its own **Remove**. The app name has no reset; clear the field and save to drop it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are these settings visible to everyone?">
    The branding is — the app name, favicon and colours are what every user in the account sees. Global instructions and global knowledge are not shown to users; only the answers assistants give using them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
