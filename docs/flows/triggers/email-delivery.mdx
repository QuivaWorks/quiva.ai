An Email delivery trigger starts a flow when something happens to an email your account sent. Use it to retry, notify someone, or open a task when mail fails.

This is different from the [Email trigger](/flows/triggers/email), which starts a flow when mail arrives at an issued address.

## Setting it up

Add an **Email delivery** trigger from **Add Node**, then set:

- **Start this flow when an email is**: one or more of **Bounced**, **Blocked**, **Dropped**, **Not sent**, **Delayed**, **Marked as spam**, **Unsubscribed**, **Delivered**, **Opened** and **Clicked**.
- **Email type**: **Commercial**, **Transactional** or **Any**.
- **Template**: a published template, or **Any**.

A flow takes one Email delivery trigger. Changes take effect once the flow is saved and published.

<Note>
**Opened** and **Clicked** events only arrive when [Track opens and clicks](/essentials/account/email-sending#email-delivery) is on and your sending domain is verified.
</Note>

## What the flow receives

`$.trigger` holds `msg_id`, `to`, `event`, `reason`, `template_key` and `ref` (the object you set on the [Send email step](/flows/steps/email)), plus `subject` on a failure.

Email sent by a run this trigger started starts no flow and raises no task, whatever becomes of it.

If you only want a task per failed recipient, you do not need a flow: turn on **Raise a task when an email fails** under [Email sending](/essentials/account/email-sending#email-delivery).
