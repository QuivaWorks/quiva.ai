**Home** is the landing view of a space. It is a page of cards, each drawing the space's own data: what needs you, what is coming up, recent files and so on. You arrange the cards yourself.

{/* IMAGE: S03 — space Home with cards */}

## Who can change Home

Admins of the account can customise Home and any card page. Customising is not available on mobile. Everyone else sees the page as saved.

In a space that is locked by the account that provided it, an admin can still arrange their own copy of the page.

## The default Home

Until someone saves a layout, Home shows a default set of cards. A card appears only if the space has what it needs: a contact record for the journey cards, or the Tasks, Files, Records, Meetings or Chats feature turned on.

| Where | Default cards |
|---|---|
| Space Home | **Act now**, **Today**, **Journeys by stage**, **How long they've waited**, **Team workload**, **Waiting on others**, **Your turn**, **Records**, **Tasks** |
| Client Home | **Journey**, **Tasks**, **Abbie**, **Meetings**, **Notes**, **Files**, **Contacts** |

If the space had a Stats dashboard set up before Home existed, it appears as **Stats** cards on Home until Home is first saved.

## Card pages

A space can have more pages of cards besides Home. Each is a named page in the space menu and opens at `?page=<id>`. A card page with nothing on it shows **This page has no cards yet.**

To add a card page, use **Add a page** under **Edit space → Pages and actions**. See [Menu and pages](/spaces/menu-and-pages).

## Customise a page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Customise">
    Select **Customise** (the sliders icon) in the space header. The page switches to edit mode and **Cancel** and **Save** replace it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange the cards">
    Drag a card by its bar to move it. Drag an edge or corner to resize it. Each card has **Remove** on its bar, and **Edit** where it has settings. Cards sit in one grid, so on Home and card pages there are no section headings to name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a card">
    Select **Add a card**. The panel offers **Suggested** cards built from your record sets and statuses, then a **Cards** list. **Custom figure or chart** counts or totals your records as a figure, chart or table: **Figure**, **Figure with trend**, **Over time**, **By category**, **Over time by category**, **Share**, **Breakdown table** or **Progress to a target**. Pick a card and it is added to the page. **Records** and **List** open their settings next so you can choose a record set. When a card on the page shows a record set, **Case pages** beside **Add a card** sets up or edits the page a record of that set opens on. See [Cases](/spaces/cases).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or discard">
    Select **Save** to keep the layout. A message confirms it: **Home saved.** or **Page saved.** Select **Cancel** to leave without saving. If you changed anything you are asked **Discard changes?** before the changes are lost.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* IMAGE: S04 — Customise + Add a card */}

In a locked space that has saved its own layout, **Reset to default** in the edit toolbar puts the provider's layout back.

## Card types

Which cards are offered depends on the page. For example, **By status**, **Needs you** and **Coming up** are offered on a record set's own page, and **Journey** on client Home only.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **List** | A record set's records with their filters, search and views. |
| **By status** | A count per status; picking one narrows the list. |
| **Needs you** | Records with an action open for you, then those waiting on others. |
| **Coming up** | Records with a date falling in the next 30 days. |
| **Journey** | Where this journey is, who has it and the next action. |
| **Your turn** | Rounds waiting on you, oldest first. |
| **Waiting on others** | Rounds handed to the other side, with a reminder at 3+ days. |
| **Act now** | Rounds waiting on you with their next step, and reminders for the long waits. |
| **Today** | Today's due and scheduled tasks on a timeline, with **Mark done**. |
| **Journeys by stage** | Open rounds per step, split into your turn and waiting. |
| **How long they've waited** | Open rounds by days waiting: 0–2, 3–6, 7–13 and 14+. |
| **Team workload** | Open tasks per teammate, highlighting those where it is your turn. |
| **Tasks** | The next task due, queue filters, and a small board of your statuses. |
| **Task board** | The Tasks board or list with its own saved filter, editable in place. Space pages only. |
| **Submissions** | Incoming submissions, awaiting first. Only where the space has a Submissions tab. |
| **Files** | Latest uploads and a drop zone that saves into this folder. |
| **Abbie** | Ask Abbie about this folder and pick up its recent chats. |
| **Meetings** | Record a meeting or add a bot, and see the latest recordings. |
| **Notes** | Quick Markdown notes saved as files in this folder's Notes. |
| **Records** | One record set by status, with the records that need a move. |
| **Contacts** | The people here, the primary contact and who can sign in. |
| **Stats** | Several charts in one card, on one time range. |

## The Stats card

A **Stats** card holds several charts and figures on one time range. Arrange its charts in place while customising. A card with no charts shows **No charts yet.**

Open the card's settings with **Edit** to set:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The card's heading. Blank shows **Stats**. |
| **Show a date range control** | Adds a period picker to the card's header, so viewers can change the period. |
| **Default period** | The period the card opens on: **Last 12 months**, **This year so far** or **All time**. Shown once the date range control is on. |

Only the figures set to follow the card's range change when a viewer picks a period. Figures with their own window keep it.

## Client Home

Opening a client or contact folder lands on its first client page, or on the client's Home if none is set. See [Entities and contacts](/spaces/entities-contacts).

Client Home shows the layout saved for client folders in the space. Until one is saved it shows the default cards above, with **Journey** across the top. A saved layout applies to every client in the space.
