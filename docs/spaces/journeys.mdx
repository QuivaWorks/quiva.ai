A **journey** is a form whose steps a client and your team work through together. Each step has an
owner, a short hint, and one or more buttons. When your team finishes a step the work moves on, and
the journey shows who it is waiting on.

Use a journey for work that has a known order and goes back and forth: onboarding a new supplier,
setting up a client project, a yearly check-in. For one-off to-dos, use a [task](/spaces/tasks).

<Note>
A journey is not a [form flow](/forms/form-flows). A flow cuts one form into sections that Abbie asks
about in a conversation. A journey is a list of steps, each with an owner and buttons, that a
specific client moves through over days or weeks.
</Note>

---

## Rounds

Each time you start a journey for a client you start a **round**. A client can have several rounds
over time, one after another, but only one open round per journey. Every round shows:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Next step** | The step the round is on now. |
| **Waiting on** | Who has the step, and for how long, for example "Waiting on Sam · 3 days". Empty when the step names no one. |

A step that is waiting on your team shows a **Your turn** pill. Home has a **Your turn** card for
the rounds with you and a **Waiting on others** card for the rounds you have handed off. See
[Home and cards](/spaces/home-and-cards).

## Build a journey

You build a journey in the form editor, on the form of a record set. Reach the editor from
**Manage Record Sets**, as described in [Building a form](/forms/building-a-form).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Journey tab">
    In the form editor header, switch from **Editor** to **Journey**. The tab is offered to
    root users, administrators and developers only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the form a journey">
    A form with no journey says **This form has no journey**. Click **Make this form a journey**. It
    starts with one step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and order the steps">
    The **Steps** column on the left lists the steps. Use **Add a step** (or **Add a step after
    this one**), then move a step up or down, or remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up each step">
    Select a step and fill in its settings on the right. The middle shows a preview of a round on
    that step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how it starts, then save">
    Under **Starting it**, give the journey a **Name** and choose what to **Suggest it for**. Then
    **Save** the form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* IMAGE: S18 — journey designer in the form editor */}

### A step's settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The step's name as people see it. |
| **Heading** | A different heading for the step's screen. Defaults to the title. |
| **Phase** | An optional group the step belongs to. |
| **Who does it** | Who the step waits on, for example your team or the client. |
| **Shown as** | The name shown in **Waiting on**. Defaults to who does it. |
| **Hint** | A line of guidance on the step. |
| **Button text** | The label of the button that finishes the step. Defaults to the last button. |
| **Buttons** | What can be done on the step, pressed in order. The last one finishes the step. |
| **Skills** | Optional skill ids hinted to Abbie on this step. |
| **Advanced** | The step's raw settings. |

### Starting it

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | What **Start a journey** lists the journey as. |
| **Suggest it for** | **No suggestion**, **A first round**, or **A later round, after one has finished**. The suggested journey is picked first when someone starts a journey in a client folder. |

## The Journey tab

Once a space has a record set with a journey, a client's folder gets a journey tab. Its name is
whatever the journey is called for your space; the default is **Journey**. It shows the client's
rounds and, for an open round, the steps with the current one marked.

## Start a journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client list">
    In the space, open the page that lists your clients (contacts or organisations, as configured
    for your space) and click **Start a journey**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who">
    Search for the client, or use **New** to add one. If your space has more than one journey,
    choose which.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Click **Start** followed by the journey's name. You land on the client's journey tab, on the new
    round.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* IMAGE: S17 — Start a journey dialog */}

<Note>
If the client already has a round open on that journey, the dialog says **[name] already has a round
open, at "[step]"** and offers **Open it**. Finish that round before starting another.
</Note>

## Steps that involve many people

Some clients have **members** filed under their folder: the people at a supplier, or the team on a
project. A members step works with all of them.

On a members step, the journey lists each member with a status pill, a **Search members** box and
paging. For each member you can open their forms, see their answers, or fill a form in with them.

Three actions run across every member:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Send to everyone** | Gives each member a task with the step's forms, creates a login for anyone without one, and invites them. Safe to run again. **Send again to everyone** repeats it; **Only people not sent to yet** skips those already sent to. |
| Preview | Before sending, shows how many members it will reach, how many will get a new login, and how many have no email address. **Who gets what** lists each member. |
| **Remind everyone waiting** | Sends a reminder to each member who has not finished. A member reminded in the last day is skipped. You can also use **Send [name] a reminder** on one member. |

Sending runs in the background. Close the dialog and it carries on; if some members fail you can
**Retry** them or **Carry on** an interrupted send. Failed members are listed under **Not sent**.

## Ask Abbie

Abbie can explain a journey and change it for you. Ask her:

- "What are the steps in the supplier onboarding journey, and who is each waiting on?"
- "Reword step 2 and add a step after it for contract review."
- "Set up a journey for onboarding a new client project: kickoff, access, first review."

She proposes the change and waits for an administrator's approval before applying it. She shows what
a round on a retired step will read as. Abbie does not start a round: use **Start a journey** for
that. Step prompts and review areas are still edited in the form editor.

## The API

Members steps are also available over the API, for staff:

| Call | Use |
|---|---|
| `POST /records/member-rows` | Get or create a member's row in a record set. |
| `POST /records/member-send` | Send, preview or remind every member under a folder, as a background job. |
| `GET /records/member-send/{id}` | Read a job's progress and its failed members. |

See the Records section of the API reference for the request and response fields.
