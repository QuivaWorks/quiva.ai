An account can **publish products** to other accounts it links with. The account that publishes is the **publisher**. The linked account is the **distributor**. Each side works in its own Space. The distributor starts requests on a product, the publisher answers them, and both sides see where each request stands.

Linking accounts, invitations and the status mapping are covered in [Service desk](/spaces/service-desk). This page covers what a linked Space shows once a product is in use.

<Note>
The screens on this page use fixed labels, set by the product: **Quotes**, **Policies**, **Needs you**, **Linked accounts** and **Submissions**. They are quoted here as they appear. A product can change the words of some stages and buttons, so a label on your screen may differ from the default quoted here.
</Note>

## What a linked Space shows

| Side | Where | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Distributor | **Quotes** tab | A list of every request on the products you are granted, with its stage |
| Distributor | **Policies** tab | The records created once a request is accepted. The tab takes the name the product gives its record set, and falls back to **Policies** |
| Distributor | **Needs you** list | What is waiting on you, at the top of the Space's **Home** |
| Publisher | **Linked accounts** tab | Your products, the accounts linked to each, and their invitations |
| Publisher | **Submissions** tab | An inbox of what linked accounts have sent you |

A distributor's Space is cut down to this work. **Records**, **Linked accounts**, **Submissions**, **Chats** and **Meetings** are not shown in it.

On a publisher's Space, **Linked accounts** and **Submissions** appear at the root of the Space. You can hide either one: in **Edit space**, open **General** and tick it under **Hidden tabs**. **Quotes** and **Policies** are not on that list. See [Spaces](/spaces/spaces) and [Menu and pages](/spaces/menu-and-pages).

## Start a request from a product

{/* IMAGE: S22 — distributor Quotes panel */}

The **Quotes** tab lists each request with its client, reference, product, **Stage** and when it was last updated. Use the search box (**Search client or reference**), the **Stage** filter and the product filter to narrow the list. Switch between a table and cards with the display switch. If the Space has no products, the tab says **This space is granted no products.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the create button">
    The button at the top right of the **Quotes** card is named by the product.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose who it is for">
    If the product files its requests against a client, a dialog titled **Who is this quote for?** opens. Search for one and select it, or choose **New** followed by the Space's word for a client, or **Start without** one. The wording follows the contact label as configured for your Space.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    The new request opens as a page with the product's form on the left. A **Premium** card sits beside it, and a picker for the contact on your side when the product asks for one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the price">
    Select **Get a price** to see a price for the answers so far. Nothing is submitted. The button stays off until the required answers are in, and while the form is locked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The price breakdown

{/* IMAGE: S23 — new request with price breakdown */}

When the product can price a request, the **Premium** card shows the result. A price that comes with lines shows them one by one, in the order the card lists them, and a combined total. A price that needs a closer look shows an alert instead, with its reasons. If your answers change after a price, the card says **Answers changed since this price**.

While the price is being worked out, the card shows **Pricing…** and updates on its own. If it fails, it shows **No price** and the reason.

### Submit

Once the required answers are in, a **Submit** button appears on the draft's status card. The **Next steps** card lists what comes after, and says they are **Available once the quote is saved and submitted.** A saved request stays a **Draft** until you submit it.

## Status and whose move it is

Every request carries a **Stage**. The stages, in order, with their default words:

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Not yet sent |
| **With the underwriter** | Sent, waiting for the publisher. The publisher's name replaces the default wording where the product supplies it |
| **Referred** | The publisher needs more information or a closer look |
| **Quoted** | The publisher has issued a price |
| **Declined** | The publisher has refused it |
| **Withdrawn** | The publisher has taken its price back |
| **Binding** | You have accepted the price and are waiting for confirmation |
| **Policy** | Confirmed |
| **Cancelled** | Ended |

A request also shows **Unsent changes** when your answers have changed since the version the publisher holds. Send them again to get a new answer.

The **Needs you** list on **Home** collects what is waiting on you, most pressing first, with the client, the reference and how long ago it moved. Each row has a button that opens the request. It shows **Nothing needs you right now.** when it is empty, and **+N more** when the list is cut short.

## After a decision

{/* IMAGE: S24 — Policies panel */}

The publisher's answer is mirrored back onto your request, so you do not enter it twice. On the request page, the **Outcome** card shows:

- The price, under **Premium**, and the dates it applies to.
- **Reason** or **Reasons** when the answer is a referral or a refusal, otherwise **Notes**.
- **Asked by the underwriter**, when the publisher has asked for more. Answers to check again are marked. Edit the request, answer them and send it back.
- **Terms**, and **Documents** the publisher has shared with you.

The action beside the form follows the stage. Before a price you send the request. After a price you can accept it, unless your answers have changed, in which case you send them for a new price first. Once a request is confirmed it appears under **Policies**, where later change and cancellation requests are sent to the publisher for a decision. One request at a time can be with the publisher.

If the publisher suspends or ends the link, existing requests stay readable but nothing new can be sent.

## The publisher's Submissions inbox

{/* IMAGE: S25 — publisher Submissions inbox */}

Everything a linked account sends arrives in **Submissions**. The table lists **Age**, **Kind**, **Quote reference**, the sender, **Product** and **Decision**. Kinds are **Rate**, **Bind**, **Change (MTA)** and **Cancellation**. Use the filters, the **Quote reference** search and the sort menu to narrow the list, and **Refresh** to read it again.

To act on a submission:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Select a row. The **Decision** area opens with three tabs: **Submission** (what was sent, with its answers and what changed since the last version), **Decision** and **Activity**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what to tell the sender">
    On the **Decision** tab, pick an outcome. Which outcomes are offered depends on what was asked: a price for a rate, an acceptance for a bind, an approval for a change or a cancellation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save or issue">
    **Save draft** keeps it to yourself, shown as **Draft — not sent**. **Issue decision** sends it, and the sender sees it on their request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An issued decision is never edited. To change one, use **Issue correction** or **Withdraw decision**. Each is a new decision.

The inbox also offers **New quote**, so you can start a request on behalf of a linked account.

## Publisher branding on the invitation page

{/* IMAGE: S21 — branded invitation page */}

When you send an invitation, the page the other account opens shows your logo, or your name when there is no logo, with an accent colour rule. Invitation and confirmation links use your published address. Invitations themselves are covered in [Service desk](/spaces/service-desk).
